## 1. Executive summary

DANA is a Mexican fashion company with more than **60 years** of experience designing and manufacturing **jackets, coats, vests, and suits** for men and women. Today, **Liverpool** (Mexico’s largest department store group) is DANA’s primary commercial partner. DANA sells under its own **DANA** brand at Liverpool and has historically operated **licensed lines** and **private-label / manufacturing-for-client** relationships.

A strategic opportunity has emerged: become an **official manufacturing supplier** for **Liverpool’s own women’s brands** — **MAP**, **KUR.KUMA**, and **PETITE STUDIO** — for outerwear (blazers, chamarras, chalecos, suéteres; and **abrigos** for KUR.KUMA) targeting the **2027** season.

Indicative commercial scale is approximately **300–600 units per design per season**, which would represent a meaningful **business extension** beyond DANA’s current branded assortment.

**Engagement objective with consultants:** Design and stand up a **Mexico-based design + manufacturing capability** so DANA can present **samples, landed costs, and a credible supply plan** to Liverpool by **September–October 2026**, with a target of **official supplier status for coats and jackets** under Liverpool’s own brands for **2027**.

## 3. Current relationship with Liverpool

### 3.1 How DANA works with Liverpool today

DANA participates across four commercial divisions:

| Division | Focus | 2025 signal |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Damas (512)** | Women’s coats, gabardinas, outerwear | **Strong growth** (+55% revenue vs 2024); ~11% net margin |
| **Abrigos Caballero (241)** | Men’s coats | Margin stress, slow rotation, high markdowns |
| **Sacos Caballero (246)** | Men’s suits/blazers | Volume decline; very slow rotation |
| **Casual Caballero (271)** | Casual outerwear | Positive margin; excellent rotation |

**Implication:** The 2027 own-brand opportunity is **women’s-led**, aligned with DANA **Damas** strength — not a repositioning of challenged men’s sacos/abrigos lines.

### 3.2 Production model today

| Partner / asset | Role |

| **DANA (in-house)** | Limited own production; Gerber-linked pattern workflow |
| **Fabric supply** | e.g. Cheres-Dixa — **3–4 month** lead times on some programs |

### 3.3 Commercial & cash context

- Liverpool pricing uses negotiated markup factors, bonifications, and CEDI assumptions.
- **Sep–Oct** concentrates a large share of Liverpool revenue; production is paid ahead of settlement — any scale-up requires **working capital** planning.
- 2026 production plan is intentionally **leaner** (~5,000+ units) with improved unit economics vs 2025.

# ANNEX A — Liverpool brand DNA (MAP, KUR.KUMA, PETITE STUDIO)

*Source: Liverpool infographics (`3 MAP_Infografía_ADN_ƒ.pdf`, `KUR_Infografía_ADN_ƒ.pdf`, `PS_Infografía_ADN_ƒ-comprimido.pdf`) in `DANA/data/`.*

## A.1 KUR.KUMA

**Purpose:** Reference brand for **sophisticated trend**; entry price toward luxury/designer; bridge Young → Contemporary Woman.

**Client:** Higher purchasing power; invests in quality wardrobe; exclusive shopping experiences; elegance via silhouette and textile (not overt sexy fit).

**Collection architecture:** 30% timeless basics · 50% collection · 20% fashion items. Small, color-coordinated collections; boutique display.

**Outerwear categories:** Chamarras · Saco/Blazers · Chalecos · Suéteres · **Abrigos**

**Materials:** High quality; natural fibers preferred; crepe, jacquard, brocade, pleats; silk-touch satin, linen look, PU, suede, pan, premium polyester.

**Fit:** Flattering — structured through oversize.

**Yes:** Elegant lengths, careful finishes, abstract muted prints, subtle transparency, sophisticated necklines, casual + formal.

**No:** Cut-outs/revealing necklines, very short garments, loud prints, bright colors, heavy distress, **sudaderas, pants, joggers**.

**DANA focus:** Premium execution; Preslow + Tramex for abrigos tier; boutique QC.

---

## A.2 PETITE STUDIO

**Purpose:** Mexico’s reference for **ideal fit for petite women**.

**Client:** Fit-first; versatile casual-to-formal; unique colors and exclusive prints; detail-oriented, chic (not childish).

**Sub-lines:** **Collection** (~72–85%) · **Sélection** (elevated formal, differentiated label) · **Privée** (capsules) · **Essentiels** (~6%).

**Materials:** Viscose, silk-touch/satin poly, delicate lace, pan, fine knit, chambray, cotton elastane; balance light vs heavy fabrics.

**Fit (critical):** Tighter armholes, shorter sleeves/hem, reduced torso, adjusted rise — **no alterations needed**.

**Prints:** All-over preferred; muted; **small–medium** scale to elongate petite figure.

**Yes:** Chic discreet habilitations; covered buttons; fine embroidery.

**No:** Excessively sexy, infantilizing, low quality, literal exaggerated trends, urban baggy/distressed/chunky.

**DANA focus:** Dedicated petite grading; fit approval on petite blocks; Preslow with pattern room discipline.

---

## A.3 MAP

**Purpose:** **Style and inspiration** reference for **Mexican women**. Proudly Mexican — designed by and for us.

**Client:** Active social/work life; confident; adapts trends; practical color/texture mix; accessories-driven; communicates **power and leadership**.

**Collection architecture:** **84% Colección** · 8% Básico · accessories/categories split (swim, intimates, etc. — out of DANA coat/jacket scope unless requested).

**Outerwear categories:** **Blazers · Chamarras · Chalecos · Suéteres**

**Materials:** Quality cotton, piyú, polyester, velvet, lace, suede; excellent drape.

**Fit:** Body-stylizing; **inclusive sizing**; trend-adapted silhouettes.

**Style:** Basics + contemporary chic — wardrobe to statement.

**Yes:** Flattering silhouettes; reinterpreted/abstract prints; **subtle licenses**; sophisticated trend; soft textiles with branded details; protagonist habilitations (buttons, lurex, studs).

**No:** Childish licenses, matronly pieces, very revealing, poor quality, literal exaggerated trends, very juvenile looks.

**DANA focus:** **Competitive trend costing** at volume; inclusive grades; Preslow; strongest unit potential per design.
