# GWEP Cumbres Herradura - One Pager (Recommendations and Takeaways)

Date: 2026-05-05

## Business Snapshot

- Leads generated (period): **10,413**
- Paid investment (Meta + Google): **$2,754,673 MXN**
- Approx. CPL: **$264.5 MXN**
- Closed-won proxy: **40**
- Approx. CAC: **$68,866.8 MXN**
- Lead-to-sale rate: **0.38%**

## Main Takeaways

1. **Top-of-funnel volume is not the main issue.**  
   The core constraint is progression from lead to sales-qualified stages.

2. **Efficiency degraded over time.**  
   Last 24 weeks vs first 24 weeks: CPL increased from $229.7 to $307.4 MXN while lead->sale moved down (0.42% to 0.34%).

3. **Paid social drives scale but low deep-funnel progression.**  
   "Redes sociales de pago" has the highest volume, but only ~0.5% reached advanced stages in the current stage proxy.

4. **Contact operations improved recently, but downstream conversion still lags.**  
   Contact rate improved materially in later weeks, yet this has not translated into proportional win growth.

## Priority Recommendations (Next 30 Days)

1. **Shift budget toward quality cohorts**
   - Reallocate 15-25% of spend from lowest progression ad sets to higher progression audiences/campaigns.
   - Keep paid social volume, but gate scaling by lead->visit and lead->apartado thresholds.

2. **Upgrade lead qualification at capture**
   - Mandatory fields: financing plan, budget range, purchase timeline, preferred unit type.
   - Route high-intent leads to fast-response queue.

3. **Implement strict speed-to-lead process**
   - First outreach in <10 minutes for paid leads.
   - 3-attempt cadence in first 24 hours by call + WhatsApp + email.

4. **Separate campaign objectives by intent**
   - TOFU campaigns (awareness/traffic) should not be judged by same KPI as high-intent conversion campaigns.
   - Build at least 2 conversion-focused campaign clusters per channel.

5. **Enforce weekly source-quality governance**
   - Weekly review by source/campaign:
     - lead->contact
     - contact->visita
     - visita->apartado
     - apartado->ganado

## Suggested 6-Week Targets

- Lead->contact: **>=10%**
- Lead->sale: **>=0.55%**
- CAC: **< $55,000 MXN**
- CPL: maintain **<= $270 MXN** while improving quality metrics

## Data Gaps to Resolve This Week

- Standardize lifecycle stage taxonomy for true "closed-won" and "closed-lost".
- Add/validate revenue per closed customer for true ROAS and payback analysis.
- Break down "Fuentes sin conexion" into actionable source buckets.

## Bottom Line

GWEP can unlock near-term growth by optimizing for **funnel progression quality** rather than raw lead volume. The next upside likely comes from better lead filtering, faster contact execution, and budget reallocation toward cohorts with stronger deep-funnel movement.
