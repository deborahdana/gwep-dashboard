## Slide 6 - What to Do in Next 30 Days

1. **Rebalance spend by quality, not just CPL**
   - Keep volume channels, but shift 15-25% to highest progressing cohorts.

2. **Install strict lead qualification at capture**
   - Budget range, timeline, financing readiness, preferred unit.

3. **Speed-to-contact SLA**
   - First contact under 10 minutes for paid leads.

4. **Campaign structure by intent**
   - Separate TOFU traffic campaigns from high-intent conversion campaigns.

5. **Create weekly source-quality dashboard**
   - Track lead->contact, contact->visit, visit->apartado by source and campaign.

## Slide 8 - Risks and Data Gaps

- Revenue/ROAS fields are not consistently populated in current source files.
- "Closed-won" may exist under stage labels outside a single standardized field.
- Large "Fuentes sin conexion" bucket needs decomposition to be actionable.

Immediate data hygiene fixes:
- Standardize stage mapping (won/lost/open)
- Enforce source/campaign naming taxonomy
- Add customer revenue and margin fields at close

---

## Slide 9 - Executive Summary

- The business has **lead volume**, but conversion depth is the limiting factor.
- Current paid mix is heavily Meta-weighted and likely over-optimized for top-funnel volume.
- Fastest growth path is **quality controls + response speed + intent-based campaign design**.
- Prioritize optimization where leads advance to visit/apartado, not only where CPL appears lower.
